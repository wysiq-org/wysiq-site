# Community Edition and Enterprise Edition

WysiQ Community and WysiQ Enterprise share the same analytical workflow.

## Community Edition

Designed for analysts. It focuses on local files, visual analysis, AI-enriched analytical knowledge, WysiQueries and sharing.

## Enterprise Edition

Designed for information systems. It builds upon Community Edition with enterprise database connectors, enterprise application connectors, analytical dataset publishing, Business Intelligence integration and enterprise database publishing.

Enterprise Edition closes the analytical loop by publishing reusable AI-enriched analytical datasets back into enterprise information systems and Business Intelligence platforms.
