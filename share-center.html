Repository documentation (English). Content may describe planned features.

Share Center

The Share Center publishes analytical knowledge. It is not only an export menu.

Interactive HTML

Graphs can be published as standalone interactive HTML pages. Recipients can zoom, pan, inspect curves and explore the graph in a browser. No WysiQ installation is required.

Social sharing

Analytical work can be shared through Telegram, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord, Email, GitHub, websites and internal knowledge portals.

Reports

WysiQ can publish PNG images, PDF reports and PowerPoint presentations.

Exchange formats

WysiQ also publishes analytical work through JSON-based formats: WysiQuery (.wysiq), WysiAQuery (.wysia) and Knowledge Packs.

WysiQ does not only exchange screenshots of analyses. It exchanges the analyses themselves.
