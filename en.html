The workflow

One continuous chain, from raw data to decision.

These steps are usually scattered across several tools, exports and scripts — and the context of the analysis gets lost along the way. In WysiQ they follow one another in a single saved workspace, with the graph as the common thread.

  1. 01AccessFiles, databases, WWW catalog
  2. 02PrepareSQL Assistant, working tables

  5. 05ReasonTests, PCA, Rules & Vars
  6. 06SimulateScenarios and constraints
  7. 07ExplainAI on computed facts
  8. 08ShareHD exports, BI tables
What you can do

Explore, analyze, simulate — right inside the graph.

01 · CONNECT

All your sources in one graph.

Build a graph from tables of entirely different origins — no preliminary merging, no data consolidation.

  • Local files, database servers and Internet data providers, side by side.
  • Multi-domain WWW catalog: energy, weather, economy, environment, health… (RTE, ENTSO-E, INSEE, Eurostat, ECB, Open-Meteo, NASA POWER…).
  • SQL Assistant to reduce and align large datasets before analysis.
02 · EXPLORE

See before you aggregate.

The graph is no longer the final illustration: it is where you find the question. Move the bounds, compare another period, overlay.

  • Several graphs in one Query: actual values, trends, analytical series, scenarios.
  • Synchronized zoom and pan, overlays with adjustable transparency to reveal similarities and gaps.
  • LINE for chronology, POLAR for cycles, Values / Percent switch without touching the data.
03 · ANALYZE & SIMULATE

From observation to measurement, then to possible futures.

Select a region, quantify it, test it, turn the result into a new series — and challenge your hypotheses without overwriting the original observations.

  • Metrics, Areas and Signals: peaks, slopes, TVAR, FFT… and back to series.
  • Statistical tests, PCA, correlations launched from the graph.
  • Rules & Vars and scenarios: dynamic thresholds, capacities, simulation constraints.

Ultra-high-resolution exports

PNG and PDF beyond screen resolution, for close inspection, publishing and further exploration elsewhere.
