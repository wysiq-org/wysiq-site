01 · What you can do

Explore, analyze and simulate — no code required

WysiQ makes the graph an interactive analytical workspace: start exploring your data before building a conventional ETL pipeline.

No-Code Analytics — From Data to Discovery

Explore, analyze and simulate complex data without writing code. Interact directly with your data from the graphical workspace.

Multi-Source Graphs — No ETL, No Table Merging

Build a single graph using multiple tables from entirely different sources: local files, database servers and Internet data providers.

Combine data from different sources directly in the same graph — without ETL pipelines, preliminary table merging or data consolidation.

Advanced Analytics — Directly from Your Graphs

Discover patterns, apply business rules, run statistical tests, perform PCA and signal processing, and simulate scenarios. Select and compare periods, calculate metrics, identify relationships and turn results into new series.

Synchronized Graph Exploration

Compare graphs with synchronized zooming and panning. Overlay plots with adjustable transparency to reveal similarities and differences.

Ultra-High-Resolution Graph Exports

Create high-resolution PNG images and PDF exports for closer inspection, sharing and further exploration in external applications, beyond ordinary screen resolution.

From Graphs to Enterprise BI

Create final SQL tables with selected datasets and analytical results, ready for use by your existing Enterprise Business Intelligence tools.

AI-Assisted Analytics and Reporting

Share analysis steps, datasets, observations, metrics, statistical tests, signal computations and simulation results with AI assistants. Explore explanations, investigate hypotheses and prepare assisted analytical reports.
