AREA PALETTE — Selection statistics, post-its, PCA and Identify

Access: Area button of the graph window or Ar circle of the Graph Remote.
Typical workflow:
  tick A in Series → draw a rectangle on the graph (Area mode) →
  Refresh → (Stats / Pct / PCA / Identify + Fit) → Save (named area) →
  Post-it or → AI.
Role
Computes the statistics of the series whose A toggle = ON, over a
selection rectangle drawn on the graph. The Area palette is also the
entry point to PCA analysis and to Identify / Fit shape exploration.
Statistical tests are no longer embedded here: they have been moved to
the Statistical Tests window (see the stats_tests_window help).
The input series are those selected with the A toggle in the Series
palette. Area therefore uses the same series for area computations,
PCA and Identify.
TOOLBAR (first row)
Dec       Number of decimals shown in the table (0–12).
Tol       X tolerance used to pair two series on neighbouring X values.
          Set 0 for an exact match, or the typical X step (e.g. 1.0 for
          daily data) for nearest-neighbour pairing.
UF        Unit factor: multiplies the sum column when Adj is ON.
Adj       Enables/disables applying the Unit factor.
Stats     Shows the extended statistics columns (σ, NA, NA%, IQR,
          MAD, skew, kurt).
Pct       Enables the percentile columns. Clicking the "Pct" text
          (left or right click) opens a small editor for the list of
          levels (e.g. 10,25,50,75,90): OK (Enter) confirms, Close (Escape)
          closes.
PCA       Computes the principal components over the X scope of the Area.
Identify  Shows the six Identify columns (shape search).
Fit       Runs the best-model computation for each A series (Identify).
Edit      Enables visual editing of the Area rectangle on the graph. An
          Area must already exist (drawn on the graph or recalled from
          an Xmin/Xmax/Ymin/Ymax combo). Drag the inside to move;
          pull the edges or corners to resize. The bound fields and the
          table stay in sync while moving.
          After a change, Save stores a new named Area;
          Refresh updates the statistics.
BOUNDS HEADER (second row)
Xmin / Xmax   Editable X range. Press Enter or leave the field
              to apply. For a date, clicking the label opens the
              calendar (OK / Cancel). These are combos: selecting a saved
              area (e.g. "A2 | value") restores its four bounds.
ΔX            Width of the selection on the X axis.
Ymin / Ymax   Y range of the rectangle.
ΔY            Height of the selection on the Y axis.
Zoom In / Out and Save
Edit       Enables visual editing of the Area rectangle on the graph (see
           also the toolbar). Drag the inside to move;
           pull the edges or corners to resize.
Zoom In    Pushes the bounds of the selected area into the X/Y bounds of
           the Series palette and forces a Plot. While Zoom In is active,
           selecting another saved area applies it to the graph.
Zoom Out   Restores the previous full-frame bounds.
Save       Stores the four current bounds. If they are new,
           WysiQ creates a named area (A1, A2, A3…) in the query JSON.
           Right-click a bound combo: Delete Area removes the displayed
           area.
ACTION BAR (right)
Refresh    Reapplies the rectangle bounds and refreshes all the
           statistics. The computation is not automatic, to avoid
           slowdowns on large data sets: click Refresh after any change
           of bounds, scope or selection.
PCA→Series Creates the temporary lambda series matching the PCs (see the
           PCA section).
Actions    Combo grouping the secondary actions:
             • Post-it     Freezes a snapshot of the stats as a floating
                           post-it.
             • Hide/Show   Hides / restores all open post-its.
             • Add Fit     (Identify, Curve mode) publishes the fitted curve.
             • → Scenario  (Identify, Curve mode) samples the curve
                           into Scenario control points.
           Clicking the visible "Post-it" text creates a post-it
           directly; the arrow drops down the other actions.
?          Opens this help.
STATS TABLE COLUMNS
#      Row number.
Serie  Series name (coloured as on the graph).
nb     Number of points in the rectangle.
sum    Sum of Y values (multiplied by UF if Adj is ON).
avg    Arithmetic mean.
min    Minimum Y value.
max    Maximum Y value.
Range  Spread: max − min.
σ      Standard deviation (Stats toggle).
NA     Number of missing / NaN values (Stats toggle).
NA%    Percentage of NaN values (Stats toggle).
IQR    Interquartile range Q75 − Q25 (Stats toggle).
MAD    Median absolute deviation (Stats toggle).
skew   Skewness (Stats toggle).
kurt   Excess kurtosis (Stats toggle).
Pxx    Percentile at level xx (Pct toggle; e.g. P10, P75).
When exactly two A series are active, three extra rows
appear:
  S1>S2 : Δ   Positive deltas (S1 − S2 > 0).
  S1<S2 : Δ   Negative deltas (S1 − S2 < 0).
  Δ (S1-S2)   All S1 − S2 deltas.
POST-ITS
A post-it is a borderless floating snapshot of the current table. It
does not update when the rectangle moves. Drag it by its header;
resize from the right/bottom edges; close with ✕.
"Copy" in the header copies all the data as TSV.
"→ AI" sends the snapshot to the AI.
PCA IN AREA
The PCA toggle computes the principal components over the X scope of the
Area: Xmin / Xmax select the rows used. The Area Ymin / Ymax do not
filter the PCA data; the full-graph Ymin / Ymax bound the values, to
avoid a different number of points per series before the computation.
The number of PCs is 3 by default (standardised data); it is no longer
adjustable in Parameters (the former Axes tab has been removed). The PCA
workbench (PCA button of the graph window) offers a fuller analysis. Area
adds the rows AXIS_PC1, AXIS_PC2, …, AXIS_RECONSTRUCTION_ERROR and
AXIS_DIST_PCA to the table. AXIS_DIST_PCA = sqrt(sum(PCi²)) acts as a
global distance score in PCA space. λ% is the variance explained by each
component, cumλ% the cumulative variance.
The PCA→Series button creates the matching temporary lambda series
(PC scores, reconstruction error, PCA distance) in the Series palette.
They are meant for quick exploration and plotting but are not
saved into query.json. To keep them, create a work table containing the
real series and the PCs, then open it as a query.
IDENTIFY / FIT (exploratory shape search)
Explores which mathematical shape best describes each A series in the
current Area rectangle. It is a Graph First aid — not a formal
statistical test. Fit % measures the quality of the model, not "the
probability that the data follow this law".
Distribution / Curve (mode combo)
  Distribution — fits the Y values inside the Area only (X ignored).
                 For residuals, noise or spread. Not well suited to
                 ordered profiles (age pyramid, time series): prefer
                 Curve.
  Curve        — fits Y = f(X) using the X axis of the series (time,
                 age, index…).
Fit          Computes the best model for each A series. The columns
             show "(click Fit)" until Fit has been pressed.
             After moving the rectangle, reloading the data or
             recomputing with changed values, click Fit again.
Identify columns (when Identify is ON)
  IdType       "dist" or "curve".
  Best model   Name of the best ranked candidate.
  Fit %        Quality score (R²-based for Curve, KS-based for
               Distribution).
  Param 1..3   Up to three fitted parameters (label=value).
Add Fit / → Scenario actions (Actions combo, Curve mode only)
  Add Fit      Publishes the best fitted curve as a new series.
               The model is evaluated over the WHOLE X of the series — values
               outside the Area rectangle may be extrapolated.
  → Scenario   Samples the best curve into Scenario control points
               and opens the Scenario palette.
  Both actions are hidden when Distribution mode is chosen.
KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
Ctrl+C     Copies the selected rows (or all) of the Stats table as
           TSV.
TIPS
• Draw a new rectangle by click-dragging on the graph in Area mode
  (Graph Remote ▸ Area).
• To move or resize an existing rectangle, click Edit then
  handle the rectangle directly on the graph.
• Use consistent Xmin/Xmax between post-its to compare
  periods.
• Set Tol to the X axis step to align series sharing the
  same dates.
• Pyramids, cohort profiles, seasonal shapes: use Curve, not
  Distribution.
• Allow ~8 points in the Area for Distribution, ~5 for Curve.
• Identify results are included in the AI Feed JSON when Identify
  is ON and Fit has been run.
SELECTION BEYOND THE FRAME (several graphs)
Area filters the points on X and Y at the same time: a Y value outside
the bounds is excluded from the "nb" count, even if its date is selected.
When drawing the Area, extending the gesture ABOVE or BELOW the frame
extends Ymin/Ymax to the extremes of the A series and of the series of the
other graphs of the same query. A gesture to the LEFT or RIGHT of the frame
extends Xmin/Xmax. The axis not exceeded keeps the drawn selection (for
example, the time peak on X). Graphs and their zoom are not changed; only
the Area rectangle takes these bounds after the mouse is released.
