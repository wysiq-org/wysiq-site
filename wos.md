# WysiQ Open Specifications

WOS means WysiQ Open Specifications.

WOS describes reusable analytical objects with open JSON-based structures.

Current object families include Dataset, Table, Graph, Metric, Scenario Metric, Area, Signal, Test, PCA, AI Profile, AI Session, WysiQuery, WysiAQuery, Knowledge Pack and Share Object.

The long-term objective is to make analytical knowledge exchange as natural as exchanging documents or source code.
