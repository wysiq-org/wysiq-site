Signals

Signals represent measured or derived analytical series.

They may be raw source series, derived series, metric outputs, moving averages, differences, ratios, PCA component series, manual trace signals, scenario signals or anomaly signals.

Signals can be visualized, compared, tested, sent to AI Sessions and included in WysiQueries.
