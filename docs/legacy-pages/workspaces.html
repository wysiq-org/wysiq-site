Workspaces

A workspace is an independent analytical environment.

It contains not only the analytical objects themselves, but also all the resources required to reproduce, extend and share an analysis.

A workspace may contain:

  • datasets
  • data sources
  • SQL Builder recipes
  • graphs
  • overlays
  • snapshots
  • PowerPoint presentations
  • metrics
  • scenarios
  • signals
  • areas
  • tests
  • PCA models
  • Expert Studio sessions
  • AI Sessions
  • AI Profiles
  • WysiQueries (.wysiq)
  • WysiAQueries (.wysia)
  • shared HTML reports
  • exported PDF reports
  • reusable Knowledge Packs

Multiple independent workspaces can coexist on the same computer.

A workspace may represent:

  • a customer
  • a project
  • a department
  • a study
  • a demonstration
  • a benchmark

Workspaces are independent, but not isolated.

Through the built-in catalogs, users can browse another workspace and duplicate reusable analytical objects with a simple click.

This makes analytical knowledge reusable across projects without copying entire workspaces.
